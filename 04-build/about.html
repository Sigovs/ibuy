Why iBuy

We cut out
the middleman.

iBuy is the direct-to-consumer inventory procurement division of Marshall Goldman Motor Sales & Leasing (“Marshall Goldman”). The purpose of iBuy is simple, we are an industry leading company which purchases high-end / collectible vehicles directly from people like you, the consumer. Whether you own your car for cash, lease your vehicle or financed by a bank, iBuy will purchase your car today. Why do we need so many cars? Founded in 1978, Marshall Goldman is the largest Luxury, Exotic and Collectible dealer in the country. We are constantly in search of high-quality vehicles to add to our remarkable inventory, we’ll pay top dollar for your car with absolutely no risk to you!

Why will we pay more than others? Other competitors in the industry are essentially brokers, they’ll buy your car, mark it up for a profit and then sell it to a dealer like Marshall Goldman. At iBuy we cut out the middleman and your car is purchased directly by the source, capturing you more money. Rest assured, it will be a simple and easy process selling your car to the industry leader. We’ve been in business since 1978!

Our story

Driven by
passion.

Marshall Goldman Motor Sales has served the automotive enthusiast community for more than 43 years. Founded by Marshall Goldman in 1978 as an automobile wholesaler and small retailer, he set out to build a business based on honesty, excellent customer service and an unwavering commitment to carrying the finest cars available. Now 40 years later with Marshall’s son Harlan at the helm, we have grown to be the preeminent dealer of preowned collectible, exotic and luxury automobiles in North America. Whether you are looking for a collectible investment grade vehicle, a modern highline Ferrari or Porsche, or a daily driver from Land Rover or Mercedes-Benz, we will have the highest quality, lowest mileage, and best optioned vehicle for you.

How it started

Meet Danny Baker.

Founder, President and CEO

Danny and his team have decades of experience buying cars from private individuals just like you. Danny has been involved in the car business at both the manufacturer franchise and the independent dealer level for over 20 years. Danny has worked for New Car brands such as: Aston Martin, Bentley, Cadillac, Chevrolet, Lamborghini, Lotus, Maserati, Mazda, Mercedes-Benz, Rolls Royce, and more.
