Our story

Driven by
passion.

Marshall Goldman Motor Sales & Leasing was founded by Marshall Goldman in 1978 on a single standard — honesty, real service, and an unwavering commitment to the finest cars in the world. More than four decades on, that standard still decides every car we buy and every offer we make.

Today the group sells and buys from two showrooms, and acquires vehicles from owners across the country.

How it started

Meet Danny Baker.

Founder, President & CEO

Danny Baker built his career over more than twenty years inside the most demanding marques in the world — Aston Martin, Bentley, Mercedes-Benz, Rolls-Royce — and spent six seasons racing in the Ferrari Challenge. Along the way he pioneered buying high-end cars directly from the people who own them.

In 2019 he brought iBuy under Marshall Goldman. That pairing gives the company something rare: the buying power to acquire virtually any vehicle, anywhere in the world, and the expertise to price it right the first time.
